Current questions and projects

These projects represent different stages of my development. I distinguish between an idea, a simulation, an experimental measurement, and an established result.

DEVELOPING

Time-Reversal Violation in Radiative Nuclear Beta Decay

An experimental concept using a T-odd momentum correlation among the beta particle, photon, and recoil system, with attention to electromagnetic final-state interactions and detector/systematic effects.

Read proposal →
PLANNING

Experimental Investigation of Quantum Polarization and Malus' Law

A quantitative experimental project on polarization, Malus' law, uncertainty analysis, and a possible progression toward low-light/single-photon and quantum-information experiments.

Read project →
TEAM PROJECT

Relativistic Time Dilation in Low-Momentum Muon Decay

CERN Beamline for Schools team proposal investigating the precision boundary of relativistic time-dilation measurements for low-momentum muons using experimental and computational approaches.

04 · About

A record of scientific work in progress.

I am a Grade 12 STEM student at Ambo University Special Secondary School in Ethiopia, developing interests in theoretical and experimental physics alongside computational methods.

My research interests currently connect nuclear physics, quantum mechanics, computational physics, quantum information, and eventually quantum computing.

This website is intentionally separate from my general personal portfolio. It is designed as a research archive: questions, proposals, documents, methods, project development, and future directions.
