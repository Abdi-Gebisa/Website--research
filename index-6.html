About

I am a Grade 12 STEM student at Ambo University Special Secondary School in Ethiopia. My current work combines theoretical and computational physics with independent research development.

I served as theoretical physics lead for a CERN Beamline for Schools team whose proposal studied low-momentum muon decay and relativistic time dilation. I am also developing research ideas in precision nuclear physics and experimental quantum mechanics.

This site is intentionally separate from my general personal portfolio. It is a record of my scientific questions, projects, methods, evidence, and developing research direction.
